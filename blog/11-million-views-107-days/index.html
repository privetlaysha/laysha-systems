11.1M views in 107 days, with no post scheduled by hand

The views did not come from posting more by hand. They came from a system that finds the stories, builds the posts to the brand book, publishes on a schedule and shows the team what worked yesterday.

What changed

Before, the brand ran five messengers, had no CRM, and every Reel and carousel was posted by hand. Carousels came out in a different style every week. Nobody could say which post brought yesterday's followers.

After ninety days the same team had four things it did not have before.

  1. A content engine. Bots read the sources the brand trusts and shortlist stories. The team votes in its work chat. The engine builds carousels at 1080×1350 in the brand's voice and checks them against forty rules before a person sees them.
  2. One calendar for every account and every channel, including anything someone posts by hand, so nothing goes out twice.
  3. Distribution that runs itself. One podcast becomes about thirty clips scheduled across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Threads and VK.
  4. A daily number. Each account shows yesterday's follower change next to its posts, so the team repeats what worked.

What to copy

  • Write the brand book as rules a machine can check: fonts, sizes, where text may sit, words you never use, how long a headline can be.
  • Let the team choose topics, not write them. A vote in the chat takes a minute; writing takes a day.
  • Put every post on one calendar with the follower change beside it. That is how you learn what to make next.
