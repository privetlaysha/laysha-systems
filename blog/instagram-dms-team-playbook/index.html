How to run Instagram DMs with a team of managers

A practical playbook for brands that sell through Instagram DMs with several managers: one inbox, ownership, a reply-time clock, follow-ups, and the five numbers to watch every morning.

Vitaly Laysha · October 18, 2026

Put every conversation in one inbox, give each one an owner and a clock, never send a bare price, and look at five numbers every morning: waiting, overdue, first response, new clients and sales per manager.

Once a brand has more than one person answering DMs, the problems change. It is no longer about writing a good reply. It is about knowing who owns each conversation, how long it has waited, and which manager actually sells.

1. One inbox for every channel

Instagram DMs and comments, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook and leads from YouTube comments should land in one place. Managers should be able to reply to an Instagram DM from that inbox, through the official API, without opening the app on a phone.

2. An owner and a clock on every conversation

Each new conversation is assigned to one manager. Each one has a timer from the buyer's last message. Pick a target, for example 60 minutes, and show the conversations that are past it at the top.

3. Automation answers first, a person closes

Keyword triggers, DM menus and comment-to-DM replies can answer the first question instantly: the price list, the sizes, the delivery time. The conversation then goes to a person. An assistant can draft the reply; the manager sends it.

4. Never send a bare price

In one audit, 1,034 conversations ended right after the price. Send the price with one line of value and one question back.

5. Follow-ups on a schedule

When a buyer goes quiet, the system reminds the owner after 24 hours and again after three days. The message is written by a person, not a template.

6. Five numbers every morning

  1. Conversations waiting for a reply right now
  2. How many of them are past the target
  3. Median first-response time, per manager
  4. New clients by channel
  5. Paid orders per manager, as a share of their new leads

On the team I audited, the best manager converted 46% of new leads and the weakest 12%. Those five numbers are how the owner sees that gap in the morning instead of in the quarterly report.

Questions people ask

How do you manage Instagram DMs with multiple people?

Use one inbox for all channels, assign each conversation to an owner, run a reply-time clock, and review waiting, overdue, first response, new clients and sales per manager daily.

Can you reply to Instagram DMs from a CRM?

Yes, through the official Instagram API a CRM can receive DMs and comments and let managers reply from the CRM.

Should a bot replace sales managers in DMs?

No. Automation should answer the first question instantly; a person should handle the conversation and the close.
