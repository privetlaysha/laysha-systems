We read 170,000 Instagram DMs. Here is where the sales died.

A sales audit of a seven-channel Instagram resale team: 3,455 conversations scored, 30% of new leads paid, 1,034 went silent after the price. What the numbers say and what to fix first.

Vitaly Laysha · October 18, 2026

Most DM sales are not lost to price. They are lost to silence: a reply that comes hours late, a price sent with nothing after it, and no follow-up when the buyer goes quiet.

Over three months I read every conversation a resale brand's sales team had across seven channels: Instagram DMs and comments, WhatsApp, Telegram, VK, Facebook and leads pulled from YouTube comments. About 170,000 messages in all. Then I scored 3,455 complete conversations, manager by manager, on twelve skills.

Where the sales actually died

Three places, in this order.

  1. Before the first reply. On one morning the brand had 2,206 people waiting for an answer on a single account, and 2,198 of them were past a 60-minute target. A buyer who asks for a price and waits half a day has usually bought somewhere else.
  2. Right after the price. 1,034 conversations ended the moment a manager sent the price. Nobody asked a question back, nobody offered the size, nobody wrote the next day.
  3. In the follow-up that never came. Follow-up and upselling were the two weakest skills across the whole team. The average follow-up score was under 5 out of 10.

The same team, a fourfold spread

The best manager turned 46% of new leads into paid orders. The weakest turned 12%. Same product, same prices, same traffic. The difference was almost entirely speed, presenting value before the price, and writing again when the buyer went silent.

What to fix first

If you sell through DMs and you can fix one thing this month, fix the order of these:

  1. Put every channel in one inbox with a clock on each conversation. You cannot manage a reply time you cannot see.
  2. Never send a bare price. Send the price with one line of value and one question: the size, the date, the colour.
  3. Make the follow-up automatic to schedule and human to write. The system reminds the manager at 24 hours; the manager writes the message.
  4. Score managers on their own conversations, not on how busy they look. The spread between your best and worst seller is your cheapest growth.

On this team, bringing everyone to 35% would add about 110 paid orders a quarter without one extra follower.

Questions people ask

Why do Instagram DM sales get lost?

Mostly through slow first replies, a price sent with no follow-up question, and no follow-up when the buyer goes quiet. In this audit 1,034 conversations ended right after the price.

What is a good reply time for Instagram DMs?

Set a target and measure it. This team used 60 minutes; on one morning 2,198 of 2,206 waiting conversations were past it.

How do you measure a DM sales team?

Score each manager on their own conversations across skills like speed, value, objections, closing and follow-up, and compare conversion from new lead to paid order.
